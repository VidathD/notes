# Transmission Errors

![](assets/documents/L05%20-%20Transmission%20Errors.pdf)

Impulse noise can occur from lighting strikes.

Crosstalk can occur due to induction.

In echo, signal is repeated after a very small time.

In jitter, delay of packets is irregular. This can't be managed as easily as a constant delay, which can easily be managed by using a buffer.

Attenuation
- In fibre optics, the rate of loss is much lower than other mediums. Therefore, they can be used for long distances.
- For distances that can't be directly connected, repeaters need to be installed in the middle. These need power. 
- Look into marine fibre deployments. Sharks can also bite cables. Also anchors can damage cables. There are ships that lift the cables to a certain height and allow them to be repaired/spliced.

Interference
- To reduce interference use STP cables or fibre. Some devices don't support fibre.
