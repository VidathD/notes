Superheated steam is invisible.

Reheat temperature is higher than the boiler (inlet) temperature. We do not increase boiler temperature even if it increases efficiency because it can lead to failures.
- Fatigue failures (sudden)
- Creep failure (long term)
Reheater is at lower pressure so can increase temperature without increasing risk of creep failure.

When reheater temperature is increased, dryness fraction increases. Which means fraction of water is less and vapour is higher, which is better for the turbines. Also efficiency slightly increases.

Safe dryness fraction is about 90% or higher.

Gas turbines emit high temperature air. If it is used as topping cycle, we use heat recovery steam generators to capture the heat and generate energy again, leading to a combined cycle power plant. Almost all thermal power plants are combined cycle.

Reheat pressure is usually 20-25% of boiler pressure.
