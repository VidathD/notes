## Problem-Solving Agents

Take the example of a trip. Where we are now is the initial state. Where we want to go is the goal state. We have to find a path from initial state to goal state. There can be many intermediate states in between. Different cities can be the states. The route may be determined to factors like time taken, cost, distance, etc…
